Welcome to the Wyoming Tick Data Dashboard

Data last updated: November 16, 2026
Data Disclaimer: Data are added as they become available. Tick collection records may appear before pathogen testing is complete. Because collection efforts vary across Wyoming, little or no data does not mean ticks, pathogens, or health risks are absent.
Important Note: Only ticks originating from Wyoming are included in this data dashboard. Records associated with out-of-state travel will be available in the data download once that is published.
